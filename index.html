Green Cooling Intro — with Green Concepts

Air cooling principle

Evaporative air cooling is a natural, age-old practice. Winspeed only builds climate-friendly cooling solutions — we are contributing to the world going green.

People living in the desert use various methods of evaporative cooling — where evaporation of water is used to cool the air — when temperatures soar. The practice is not new: in the old days, people would often hang water-soaked bed sheets or blankets in windows and doorways and rely on natural wind or fans to pump air through the moist cloth to cool the room.

Evaporative air coolers — a refined concept of the age-old practice — provide an alternative to refrigerated air conditioning.

Why does natural evaporation lower temperatures? Evaporation takes place throughout the day — for example, we perspire in hot weather, and the water vapour produced by sweating takes heat away from our body as it is exposed to air. Whenever dry air passes over water, the water is absorbed by the air. That is why evaporative cooling naturally occurs near waterfalls, rivers, lakes and oceans — the hotter and drier the air, the greater the water absorption.

Tomorrow's world needs environment-friendly, energy conserving solutions and products. With increasing global warming and environmental degradation, people around the world recognise that businesses must act responsibly and offer green products to customers. Winspeed enables people across the region to capitalise on eco-friendly, energy-saving air cooling technologies as a serious alternative to harmful and inefficient air conditioners.

Air coolers vs. air conditioners

Air coolers are green. Let's compare evaporative air coolers with air conditioners to find out what makes them green.

Energy-efficient

Air coolers consume up to 10 times less energy than ACs. They consume less electricity and can also work in low voltage areas. Monthly electricity bills are significantly lower.

Low maintenance

Air coolers require low maintenance; you rarely need to summon a mechanic for maintenance or troubleshooting as in the case of ACs.

Environmentally friendly

Significant environmental benefits with no harmful CFC emissions and very low power consumption. Air coolers use fresh, clean air and allow windows to stay open for better circulation — ACs recirculate stale air in a sealed room.
